📖 Detailed Step-by-Step User Guide

Step 1: Drop Your Image, Flyer, or Artwork · [Print Dropzone (#dz)]Drop your high-resolution image or design (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`) onto `#dz`.✓ Result: Source pixel dimensions and aspect ratio are inspected.
Step 2: Pick Physical Trim Size & Bleed Margin · [Trim Size (#size) & Bleed Input (#bleed)]Select the target physical sheet size in `#size` (`4x6`, `5x7`, `8x10`, `Letter`, `A4`) and set the bleed margin in inches in `#bleed` (standard commercial bleed is `0.125` in / 3 mm).✓ Result: Target physical dimensions (`4.25 × 6.25 in` with bleed) and 300-DPI pixel targets (`1275 × 1875 px`) are calculated.
Step 3: Click Prepare Print Sheet & Check DPI Verdict · [Prepare Button (#prep) & Preview (#list)]Click **Prepare (`#prep`)**. Inspect the generated proof in `#list` showing the effective DPI calculation, bleed extension area, and corner crop/trim marks.✓ Result: Visual print proof and DPI quality verdict displayed on screen.
Step 4: Export Print-Ready PDF at Exact Physical MediaBox · [Export PDF Button (#pdf)]Click **Export PDF (`#pdf`)** to generate a PDF with exact PostScript point dimensions (`72 pt = 1 inch`, e.g., `306 × 450 pt` for `4.25 × 6.25 in`) and corner trim marks.✓ Result: Print-shop-ready PDF saved locally.

🎛️ UI Controls & Shortcuts Reference

  • Physical Size (`#size`) — Presets for `4x6`, `5x7`, `8x10`, `Letter` (`8.5×11 in`), and `A4`.
  • Bleed Margin (`#bleed`) — Bleed margin in inches added to all four edges (default `0.125` in).
  • Prepare (`#prep`) & DPI Readout — Renders the sheet preview with corner crop marks and reports effective DPI.
  • Export PDF (`#pdf`) — Builds a PDF with exact physical `MediaBox` dimensions via `pdf-lib`.

💡 Pro Tips & Real-World Workflows

  • Most commercial print shops (FedEx Office, VistaPrint, local offset printers) require a `0.125 inch` (`3 mm`) bleed so white slivers don't appear when the guillotine blade trims the stack.
  • Check the **DPI effective** readout before printing: `300+ DPI` is sharp for handheld postcards/photos; `150–200 DPI` is acceptable for wall posters viewed from 3+ feet away.
  • Open the exported PDF in Adobe Acrobat or PDF Studio to verify the page size in inches matches `Trim Size + (2 × Bleed)`.
