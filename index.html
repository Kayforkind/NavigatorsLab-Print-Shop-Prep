What it does

  • DPI advisor tells you the maximum sharp print size for any image
  • 4×6 to A4 at true 300 DPI; warns before you pay for a blurry print
  • MediaBox at the exact physical size, optional bleed

How it works

  1. Step 1. Drop an image
  2. Step 2. Pick the physical size: 4×6, A4, Letter…
  3. Step 3. Read the DPI verdict, export the print-ready PDF
Private by design. Files are processed on your device and never sent to any server. No accounts, no tracking, no cookies, nothing retained. Prefer the hub? The same tool lives at https://navigatorslab.com/tools/printprep.html.
